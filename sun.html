Sun

Meaning & Use

Light descending to bring forth abundance.

In the sun’s pattern, the light is inverted and directed toward the abundance below. If the forest represents the coexistence of light and abundance, then the sun represents the process by which light descends to create abundance. The same two elements form a different relationship.

The sun motif is used around farmland or in seasonal rituals, conveying both the hope for future growth and gratitude for what has already matured.

Reading the Symbol

LIGHT ↓ ABUNDANCE
Descent → Growth

The light form is inverted and directed downward toward abundance. Its orientation expresses light descending to help fruit grow and ripen.
